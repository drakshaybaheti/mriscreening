Patient instructions

Instructions transcribed from the supplied hospital screening form; confirm against the specific scan and current local protocol.

Food and medicines

No fasting for routine MRI except MRCP or studies under sedation/anesthesia. Other patients can take prescribed medicines and a light meal.

Attendant

One attendant is compulsory for contrast-enhanced studies.

Remove metal items

Remove metal, jewellery, glasses, hairpins, dentures, hearing aids and clothing with metal buttons; wear a hospital gown.

Personal items

Keep cards, mobile phones and watches out of the MRI room.

Previous scans

Bring old scan films and CDs for radiologist review and comparison.

Waiting time

Waiting may increase due to emergencies. MRI scan time is usually 30–60 minutes depending on the study.
